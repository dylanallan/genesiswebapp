Recording a grandparent’s life story: 20 questions to ask

The stories that matter most are rarely written down. A phone and an hour with a grandparent can save them for every generation after you.

Before you start

  • Pick a quiet room and put the phone close to them, not to you.
  • Keep it short: 30 to 45 minutes is plenty. You can always come back.
  • Bring an old photo. Pictures bring back memories that questions alone don’t.
  • Let silences happen. The best answer often comes after a pause.

Childhood

  1. Where did you grow up, and what did your home look like?
  2. What were your parents like? What did they do for work?
  3. What did a normal Sunday look like when you were small?
  4. What food reminds you most of your childhood?
  5. Who were your best friends, and what did you get up to?

Growing up

  1. What was school like? Was there a teacher you still remember?
  2. What was your first job, and what did you earn?
  3. What did you want to be when you grew up?
  4. What was the biggest news event you remember from those years?
  5. Did you ever move far from home? What was that like?

Love and family

  1. How did you meet your husband or wife?
  2. What do you remember about your wedding day?
  3. What was it like when your first child was born?
  4. What traditions did you keep from your own parents?
  5. Which relatives do you wish we knew more about?

Looking back

  1. What was the hardest time in your life, and how did you get through it?
  2. What are you proudest of?
  3. What has changed the most since you were young?
  4. What do you want your great-grandchildren to know about you?
  5. Is there a story you’ve never told anyone?

After the interview

Write down the names, places and dates they mention while they are fresh, and add them to your family tree. Most of all, keep the recording itself. Years from now, hearing their voice will mean more than any transcript.

Can’t visit in person? Many relatives find it easier to answer one question at a time, when they feel like it. Send a few questions by text and let them reply in their own time.
