# Genesis Heritage: HeyGen avatar ad

Two cuts of one ad, ready to paste into HeyGen. Every claim matches what the app does today.

## Setup (HeyGen)

| Setting | Choice |
|---|---|
| Avatar | Your own Digital Twin (consented), or a warm, older-looking stock avatar. Never a real person who hasn't consented. |
| Engine | Avatar IV (expressive). Avatar V if you use your Digital Twin. |
| Voice | Calm, warm, unhurried. If using your twin, clone your own voice. |
| Language | English first. Make French and Spanish versions with Video Translator (lip-sync on). |
| Background | Soft living room, warm light, family photos on a shelf. No logos but ours. |
| Format | 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Shorts; 1:1 for Facebook feed. |
| Captions | On, burned in, large (many viewers watch muted). |
| Disclosure | When posting, answer “yes” to each platform's AI-content question (TikTok AI-generated label, YouTube altered-content setting, Meta's AI info label). No on-screen label needed. |

The avatar is the spine, not the whole ad: cut away to the app every few seconds (cutaways listed with each line). Record the cutaways as screen recordings of the live site.

## Production plan: HeyGen reference, then polish

1. **HeyGen reference video.** Your own avatar, your own voice and your chosen HeyGen style, reading the lines above. This locks the face, voice and timing.
2. **Polish in Higgsfield (or similar).** Use the reference as the base: camera moves, lighting, a warmer cinematic look and smoother motion, keeping the same face and voice. Never change what is said or add claims.
3. **Cutaways.** Screen recordings of the live app for each line (listed in the tables), plus a few gentle B-roll shots: old photos on a table, a grandmother and grandchild looking at a phone.
4. **Edit.** Captions burned in, end card with the link.
5. **Review, then post.** Watch it all the way through, check every claim against the app, and make two or three variations to test.

## Before publishing

- Watch it all the way through. Check the captions and that each cutaway matches what is said.
- When uploading, tick the platform's “made with AI” box.
- Use the waitlist link on the end card while the app is pre-launch.
- Make at least two versions (different avatars or openings) so the ads don't tire quickly.
