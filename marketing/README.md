# Genesis Heritage: pre-launch ad kit

Tagline: **"Ancestry gives you the foundation, but Genesis takes you on the journey."**

The images are in `marketing/ads/` in two sizes:
- `*-square.png` (1080×1080): Facebook and Instagram feed.
- `*-story.png` (1080×1920): Instagram Stories and Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Stories.

To change the wording, edit `marketing/ads.json` and run `node marketing/render-ads.mjs`.

## Rules that keep the ads safe

- **Only say true things about competitors.** Name Ancestry or MyHeritage only in statements that are true. Ads that name Ancestry carry a small "not affiliated" line.
- **No made-up prices for competitors.** Say "a fraction of the price", or quote their current public price on the day, with the date.
- **No fake reviews or testimonials.** Use real quotes from beta families, with their permission.
- **Claims we can back up today:**
  - free to start
  - Pro is $19 CAD a month
  - GEDCOM import and export
  - interviews in 9 languages
  - AI storybooks
  - free record search with no paywall
  - we never sell data
  - download or delete everything

## Short video scripts (15 to 30 seconds, vertical)

**1. "The question I never asked" (emotional, about 25 seconds)**
- 0–3s: Close-up of an old photo. Text on screen: "I never asked Grandma how she met Grandpa."
- 3–8s: Phone on a kitchen table. The app reads aloud: "How did you meet your husband?"
- 8–16s: A grandparent laughs and starts telling the story. Captions appear as she speaks.
- 16–22s: The answer turns into a written page in the Story Library.
- 22–25s: End card with the tagline and "Start free".

**2. "Move your tree in 2 minutes" (how-to, about 20 seconds)**
- 0–3s: "Already on Ancestry? Watch this."
- 3–12s: Screen recording: export the GEDCOM file, upload it to Genesis, "Found 1,284 people", click Add.
- 12–17s: The tree appears, then the Tree checkup finds two date mistakes.
- 17–20s: End card: "Bring your tree. Keep the stories."

**3. "Hints vs evidence" (comparison, about 20 seconds)**
- 0–4s: Text on screen: "A hint says 'maybe'."
- 4–14s: The Research assistant on screen, with ticks and crosses beside one record and an "86/100 Strong match" badge.
- 14–20s: The AI explanation scrolls past. End card: "Don't guess. See the evidence."

## Pre-launch plan (first 4 weeks)

1. **Week 1: founding families.** Post ads 08 and 01 organically on Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Ask 20 to 50 friends and family to sign up and try an interview. Collect real feedback and quotes, with permission.
2. **Week 2: interest groups.**
   - Share in genealogy and heritage communities where the rules allow it. Follow each group's rules and don't spam.
   - Target groups by heritage, such as Latino family history, Canadian genealogy and Irish roots.
   - Lead with ads 02 and 07.
3. **Week 3: small paid test.** About $5 to $10 a day on Facebook and Instagram, spread across ads 02, 03 and 05.
   - Audience: ages 35 to 70, interested in genealogy, family history and family-tree sites.
   - Keep whichever ad gets the cheapest sign-ups.
4. **Week 4: holiday hook.** Push ad 04 (storybook as a gift) and ad 02 ("record Grandma at the holidays").

Track sign-ups through the "Get started" checklist: tree started, interview recorded, relative invited, storybook made. A family that does all four is very likely to stay and pay.
