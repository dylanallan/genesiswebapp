# 30 days of posts: Genesis Heritage pre-launch

Drafts only. Nothing here is scheduled or posted. Review each one, record or design the visual, then schedule it yourself (or ask Claude to load the ones you approve into Metricool **for review**).

**Rules for every post**
- Honest: no fake reviews, no made-up numbers, no "thousands of families already…". Before launch we have no user numbers, so we don't quote any.
- Competitor prices: only quote Ancestry or MyHeritage prices after checking them on their own websites the same week. Otherwise say "a subscription" without a number.
- AI content: if a video uses an AI avatar or AI voice, switch on the platform's AI-content label when posting (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube all ask). No on-screen label needed.
- Real family photos only with the family's permission. Use your own family, or stock/illustration.
- CTA link: `genesisheritage.netlify.app/#waitlist` (put it in the bio; say "link in bio" on TikTok/Instagram).

**Pillars**
- **S**: Stories & voices
- **P**: Privacy
- **$**: Price
- **R**: Free records
- **F**: Family sharing

**Formats**
- **V**: short vertical video (TikTok / Reels / Shorts)
- **C**: carousel (Instagram / Facebook)
- **I**: single image
- **T**: text post (Facebook)

Each day lists the English version first, then French (FR). The French is written for Quebec and is not a word-for-word translation.

## Week 2: How it works

### Day 8 · R · V
- **Hook:** "How to climb your Quebec family tree, one marriage at a time."
- **Content:** A Quebec marriage record usually names both sets of parents. Each marriage gives you the couple one generation up. Women are recorded under their maiden names.
- **FR hook:** « Remonter votre arbre québécois, un mariage à la fois. »
- **CTA:** Guide (`/guides/quebec-records/` · FR `/guides/archives-quebec/`)

### Day 9 · S · V
- **Hook:** "I asked my dad one question and he talked for an hour."
- **Visual:** Real interview moment (with permission) or a re-enactment. Then show the *Interview a Relative* screen.
- **Caption:** The questions do the work. Genesis reads them aloud and saves the answer as a story in your family library.
- **FR hook:** « J'ai posé une seule question à mon père. Il a parlé pendant une heure. »
- **CTA:** Waitlist

### Day 10 · P · C
- **Hook:** "What a family history app should never do with your data."
- **Slides:** Sell it · Use your DNA or tree to train AI without asking · Make you pay to download your own tree · Lock your photos if you stop paying · "Genesis does none of these."
- **FR:** « Ce qu'une application de généalogie ne devrait jamais faire avec vos données. »
- **CTA:** Waitlist

> Only claim what is true in the app's privacy policy at the time of posting.
