# Holiday gift campaign: drafts

Drafts only. Nothing here is scheduled, posted or sent. These posts and the email can only go out after the site is live
and payments are switched on (see `LAUNCH.md`).

**What's true about gifts (keep every post to this):**
- A gift is one month or one year of Genesis Heritage Pro, paid by Interac e-Transfer or a PayPal link, in Canadian dollars.
  Check the live prices on the Plans page before posting a number.
- The gift code shows up once the payment is confirmed, **usually within one business day**. Don't promise "instant".
- The card can be printed, shared or copied. It works once and is good for two years.
- The person receiving it doesn't need to have an account yet. They make one when they redeem.
- We don't email the person receiving it. The buyer gives them the card.
- Same honesty rules as `social-month.md`: no made-up numbers, no fake reviews, AI-content label switched on for AI video.

**Gift page:** `/gift` · **Redeem page:** `/redeem`

**Order-by dates to say out loud:** since codes can take a business day, suggest buying by **Dec 22** for Christmas
and by **Dec 30** for New Year's. Post 8 below handles the last-minute buyers.
